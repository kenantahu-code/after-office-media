After Office Publisher — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 September 2026

After Office Publisher is a creator publishing service operated by After Office ID. This policy explains how information is handled when a user connects a supported social media account and uses the service to prepare or publish content.

Information we process

The service may process account identifiers, basic profile information, authorization tokens, publishing metadata, media URLs, captions, privacy and interaction selections, commercial-content disclosures, and publish-status information that are necessary to operate official platform APIs.

How we use information

Information is used to authenticate connected accounts, display current creator permissions, prepare user-approved posts, publish content after explicit approval, check publishing status, prevent duplicate submissions, troubleshoot failures, and maintain operational records.

Authorization and account access

Users connect their own accounts through official platform authorization flows. After Office Publisher does not ask for or store a user's TikTok password. Users should only connect accounts they own or are authorized to manage.

Storage and security

Access credentials are stored in restricted automation or server-side secret storage and are not intentionally committed to public repositories. Public media used for API publishing may be hosted on the After Office Publisher media site so that supported platform APIs can retrieve the files.

Sharing

Information is shared with social platforms and technical service providers only when required to provide the publishing workflow. After Office Publisher does not sell personal information.

Retention

Operational records may be retained for troubleshooting, duplicate prevention, security, and publishing history. Authorization tokens are retained only while needed for the authorized integration and may be refreshed, replaced, revoked, or deleted when access ends.

Revocation and deletion

Users may revoke platform access through the relevant social platform account settings. Requests concerning data associated with After Office Publisher can be directed to After Office ID through the official social media account @after.officeid.

Children

The service is not intended for children under the minimum age required to use the connected social platform.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the service, legal requirements, or connected platform requirements change. The current version will remain available on this page.
