After Office Publisher — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 September 2026

After Office Publisher is an internal content publishing tool operated by After Office ID. This policy explains how information is handled when an authorized operator connects a social media account to the service.

Information we process

The service may process account identifiers, basic profile information, authorization tokens, publishing metadata, media URLs, captions, and publish-status information that are necessary to operate official platform APIs.

How we use information

Information is used only to authenticate authorized accounts, publish approved After Office ID content, check publishing status, prevent duplicate posts, and maintain operational records.

Storage and security

Access credentials are stored in private automation secrets and are not intentionally committed to public repositories. Public media used for publishing may be hosted on the After Office ID media site so that platform APIs can retrieve the files.

Sharing

Information is shared only with service providers and social platforms when required to perform the publishing workflow. We do not sell personal information.

Retention

Operational records may be retained for troubleshooting, duplicate prevention, and publishing history. Authorization tokens are retained only while needed for the authorized integration and may be replaced or revoked.

Revocation and deletion

Authorized users may revoke platform access through the relevant social platform account settings. Requests concerning operational data can be directed to After Office ID through the official social media account @after.officeid.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the internal publishing workflow or platform requirements change.
