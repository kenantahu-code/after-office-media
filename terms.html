After Office Publisher — Terms of Service

Effective date: 13 September 2026

After Office Publisher is an internal publishing tool used by After Office ID to prepare and publish approved social media content to accounts owned or managed by After Office ID.

Authorized use

The service is intended only for authorized After Office ID operators. Users must only connect social media accounts they own or are authorized to manage.

Content responsibility

Users are responsible for ensuring that content published through the service complies with applicable laws, platform rules, intellectual property rights, and community guidelines.

Third-party platforms

The service connects to third-party services such as TikTok and Instagram through their official APIs. Availability and functionality may depend on those platforms and their policies.

Security

Authentication credentials and access tokens are handled only for operating authorized publishing workflows and are not intentionally exposed publicly.

Changes and availability

The service may be changed, paused, or discontinued at any time as an internal operational tool.

Contact

For questions regarding this service, contact After Office ID through the official social media account @after.officeid.
