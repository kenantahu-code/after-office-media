Publish approved creator content with fewer manual steps.

After Office Publisher is a web publishing workflow for creators and small content teams. It uses official TikTok authorization and Content Posting APIs to connect a creator account, prepare photo posts, choose available privacy settings, and send approved content for publishing.

Connect TikTok
Publishing flow
  1. Creator connects their own TikTok account.
  2. Current creator permissions are checked before publishing.
  3. Creator reviews caption, privacy, comments, disclosures, and media.
  4. Explicitly approved content is sent through TikTok's official API.
  5. Publish status is checked until TikTok returns a final result.

Built around creator control

The workflow is designed so publishing settings come from the connected creator's latest TikTok permissions and are reviewed before a post is sent.

Account authorization

Creators authorize access through TikTok Login Kit. Passwords are never requested or stored by After Office Publisher.

Publishing controls

Posts can include title, description, privacy selection, comment controls, commercial-content disclosure settings, and automatic recommended music for supported photo posts.

Status & safety checks

The service validates media URLs, checks creator information before publishing, prevents accidental duplicate submissions, and records the final publish status.

How a post moves through the service

1. Connect

Authorize the TikTok account and required scopes.

2. Prepare

Add approved photo media and publishing metadata.

3. Review

Confirm creator, privacy, comments, disclosures, and consent.

4. Publish

Send to TikTok and wait for the confirmed API result.

Important: After Office Publisher only publishes content when an authorized creator or operator has approved the post. Availability of public visibility depends on the permissions and audit status provided by TikTok.

About After Office Publisher

After Office Publisher was created by After Office ID to make multi-step social publishing more reliable. The service is intended for creators and small content teams who need a controlled workflow for approved photo content, publishing metadata, platform authorization, and final-status verification.
